# Privacy Policy — Flicker

**Last updated: August 2026**

Flicker is a local media triage app. This policy explains how it handles data.

## What Flicker accesses

Flicker reads the photos and videos in a folder you explicitly choose, so it can display
them for you to review. This may include personal photos or recordings.

## What Flicker does with that access

- All file access happens **entirely on your own device**.
- Flicker has **no network connection, no analytics, no telemetry, and no third-party
  services**. It does not send any file, filename, metadata, or usage information
  anywhere.
- There are no user accounts and nothing is uploaded to any server.
- "Deleted" files are moved to a `_deleted` subfolder inside the source folder you
  selected, not permanently erased, and not sent anywhere.
- "Moved" files go only to a destination folder on your own device that you choose.

## Data retention

Flicker does not retain any data outside the folders you point it at on your own
computer. Uninstalling Flicker does not delete your media files.
